# Magdeburg Hospital and OVGU Information

## University Medicine Magdeburg (UMMD)
The Medical Faculty of Otto-von-Guericke University Magdeburg (OVGU/UMMD) provides the complete infrastructure and expertise required to prepare, authorise, conduct, and analyse a Pre-CE (MDR Article 62) clinical investigation of an AI-based medical device in Germany.

The Koordinierungszentrum für Klinische Studien (KKS) Magdeburg functions as the institutional clinical trial centre, offering full Good Clinical Practice (GCP)–compliant services. The clinical and imaging environment of OVGU/UMMD includes advanced MRI, CT, PET/CT, and SPECT/CT systems integrated into routine clinical practice.

## Data Protection and Data Integration Center (DIZ)
Magdeburg University has established the Data Integration Center (DIC/DIZ) of the University Medicine Magdeburg and Research Data Management (RDM) resources to provide an adequate IT infrastructure.

**Data Protection:**
* All data processing is covered by approvals from the institutional ethics committee and is fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (DSGVO).
* Data will be managed within a secure, learning-ready environment based on the XNAT platform of the Data Integration Center (DIC). Access to sensitive data will be restricted and personalized, secured by authentication.
* Long-term preservation will be ensured by the DIC at the University Medicine Magdeburg, which has a mandate for sustainable data storage.

**GPU Availability (H100):**
* Compute resources combine secured, already available on-premises GPUs (H200-class) for privacy-sensitive training.
* Scaling experiments have been successfully conducted on an **NVIDIA H100 GPU Cluster**, utilizing single nodes equipped with **8x NVIDIA H100 80GB GPUs** (connected via NVLink/NCCL). These high-performance resources (e.g., from EuroHPC/JUPITER and KISSKI) are used for compute-bound and memory-intensive large-scale model training.
