Biochemical Recurrence (BCR)

Rise in PSA after curative treatment (20-50% patients) LinezRodrguezFernndez2014, GarznTorres2018.

PSA defined: ≥0.2 ng/mL (post-RP) or nadir+2 ng/mL (post-RT). Precedes clinical recurrence by months/years AmalachandranSivathapandi2024, Jadvar2016.
Often negative at BCR; limited sensitivity for small recurrences or lymph nodes RenYuan2015.
Pelvic mpMRI highly accurate for local recurrence post-RP, even at low PSA (<1.0 ng/mL) GlemserRotkopf2022.
Highly sensitive: detects recurrence earlier, at low PSA values. Locates sub-cm nodes/low-volume bone disease MolinBarry2025, KumarLawrentschuk2020.
Biopsy confirms local recurrence if indicated. Identifies residual disease VetroneMei2023.

Progression to CRPC

Disease progression despite low testosterone (≤50 ng/dL) following ADT UnknownAuthor2014, LeePark2020. Occurs within 18-24 months of ADT HuMao2023.

Diagnosis: biochemical or clinical progression + castrate testosterone. Progression: 2 consecutive PSA increases of 50%, new imaging, or worsening clinical status CruzMontijanoAmoSalas2024.
Monitors systemic progression; appearance of new lytic lesions UnknownAuthor2015, UnknownAuthor2012. Limited by non-measurable sclerotic lesions (RECIST 1.1) UnknownAuthor2014.
DWI (ADC) can monitor treatment response: transient increase in ADC with therapeutic response, persistently low ADC with resistance UnknownAuthor2014.
Mandatory for selecting patients for PSMA-directed radioligand therapy VetroneMei2023. Crucial for response assessment AmalachandranSivathapandi2024.
Tissue biopsy confirms CRPC phenotype, guides therapy. IHC for AR expression BellmuntKheoh2016.

AR-Dependent Resistance

Cancer retains addiction to AR signaling via genomic/metabolic reactivation UnknownAuthor2014, MartinCaraballo2024.

High PSA dynamics (short PSADT) indicate aggressive disease UnknownAuthor2017. Often still PSA-producing.
Imaging findings reflect disease progression (lymph nodes, metastases), but not specific to AR-dependent mechanisms.
Metabolic changes detected by MRSI (elevated choline-to-creatine ratio) can identify residual/recurrent disease KurhanewiczSwanson2002.
Increased PSMA expression correlates with higher tumor grade and disease progression VetroneMei2023, LuiningMeijer2021.
Key: AR overexpression/amplification (30% cases), AR mutations, AR splice variants (AR-V7) lack LBD. Intracrine androgen synthesis MartinCaraballo2024, UnknownAuthor2014.

AR-Independent Resistance

Progression to a phenotype entirely independent of AR signaling (APIPC) UnknownAuthor2014.

May show PSA decline or low PSA despite progression, reflecting AR-independent phenotype. Worsening clinical status UnknownAuthor2014.
Detects visceral spread more common in AR-independent disease UnknownAuthor2014.
WB-MRI offers comprehensive staging for metastases, including visceral PasoglouMichoux2016.
Pitfall: ~10% PCa shows low/absent PSMA expression VetroneMei2023. PSMA-negative lesions (often NEPC) result in false negatives Jadvar2016. FDG-PET may be adjunctive AmalachandranSivathapandi2024.
Biopsy confirms poorly differentiated tumors with neuroendocrine differentiation (NEPC). Crucial for guiding non-AR targeted treatment UnknownAuthor2014, AmalachandranSivathapandi2024.

Neuroendocrine PCa (NEPC)

Highly aggressive variant, often induced by ADT/radiation. Loss of PSA production, visceral spread MartinCaraballo2024, UnknownAuthor2014.

Characterized by low/normal PSA despite extensive disease. Poor prognosis UnknownAuthor2014. Visceral metastases, disease-related symptoms.
Detects rapid systemic progression, extensive visceral metastases (liver, lung) common in NEPC UnknownAuthor2014.
WB-MRI useful for detecting extensive visceral and bone metastases, characteristic of aggressive NEPC PasoglouMichoux2016.
Often PSMA-negative; intense FDG uptake is characteristic, alongside faint PSMA avidity, reflecting heterogeneity AmalachandranSivathapandi2024.
Definitive diagnosis: Histology shows small cell carcinoma features, neuroendocrine markers (chromogranin, synaptophysin), loss of AR/PSA UnknownAuthor2014.
