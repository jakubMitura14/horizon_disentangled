The grant listed on the Cyprus Funding Programmes Portal corresponds to the official EU Horizon Europe call: **`HORIZON-MISS-2026-02-CANCER-01` — Virtual Human Twin (VHT) Models for Cancer Research** under the **EU Mission on Cancer**.

Official PDF documentation, work programmes, application templates, and regulatory guides from the European Commission are categorized below:

---

### 1. Main Call & Work Programme Documents

* **[Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026–2027: 12. Missions (PDF)](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/wp-call/2026-2027/wp-12-missions_horizon-2026-2027_en.pdf)**
*The core call document.* Topic **`HORIZON-MISS-2026-02-CANCER-01`** (pages ~67–70) details the specific scope, expected outcomes, budget (€35M total, ~€8–9M per project), and topic-specific conditions.
* **[Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026–2027: 15. General Annexes (PDF)](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/wp-call/2026-2027/wp-15-general-annexes_horizon-2026-2027_en.pdf)**
Contains horizontal rules governing admissibility, eligible countries (Annex B), financial capacity/exclusion (Annex C), evaluation criteria and scoring (Annex D), and legal/financial setups (Annex G).
* **[Horizon Europe Work Programme 2026–2027: 1. General Introduction (PDF)](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/wp-call/2026-2027/wp-1-general-introduction_horizon-2026-2027_en.pdf)**
Strategic context and cross-cutting priorities of the 2026–2027 work programmes.
