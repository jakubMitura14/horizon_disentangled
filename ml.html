Stage 1: Clinical Foundation Models

Input Data

3D Images (MRI, PET, CT), EHR Data, Pathology

Supervisor Models

  • Ordinal Classifiers: Predict clinical scores (PI-RADS, TNM Stage) respecting their natural order.
  • Survival Regressor: Predicts Time-to-Progression (TTP), handling incomplete patient data.

Output

Clinically-validated predictions to guide the main AI.

↓
Stage 2: Isolate Key Factors with VAEs

VAE Architecture

A VAE (Variational Autoencoder) for each imaging type learns to represent the data.

Factor Separation

The model separates images into four key components:

  • Za: Anatomy
  • Zd: Disease
  • Zp: Patient State
  • Zs: Image Style / Confounders

Composite Loss

A special loss function (L_total = L_VAE + ...) ensures these factors are independent, removing artifacts like scanner type from the disease signal.

Output

A clean, purified signal of the patient's disease state, free from technical noise.

↓
Stage 3: Data Quality Assurance (OOD Detection)

Goal

Flags atypical or potentially mislabeled data for expert review to ensure high-quality training data.

Methodology

  • A secondary VAE learns the characteristics of 'normal' data from the purified Disease (Zd) and Patient (Zp) signals.
  • Atypical data is identified by high reconstruction error or its distance from normal data clusters.

Output

A highly reliable dataset for a more robust and trustworthy final AI.

↓
Stage 4: Model Disease Progression (Neural Jump ODE)

Input

The time-series of purified Disease (Zd) and Patient (Zp) signals.

NJDE Model

The NJDE models two processes:

  • 1. Continuous Evolution (ODE): Smooth disease progression over time.
  • 2. Discrete Jumps: Sudden changes from treatments like surgery or therapy.

Training Strategy

  • Pre-trained on clinical guidelines.
  • Fine-tuned on real patient data, handling missing information gracefully.

Output

A dynamic model that understands and can predict the patient's entire disease journey.

↓
Stage 5: Clinical Application & Decision Support

Generative Synthesis

Creates realistic 'what-if' images (e.g., "what would this tumor look like in 6 months?") to explain predictions.

Treatment Simulation

Simulates outcomes for different therapies to help clinicians choose the best one for the patient.

Automated Reporting

Auto-generates structured reports with predictions and simulations to save clinicians time.
